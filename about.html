About Bendele Electric

A family-owned electrical contractor in Santa Maria, California, 20 years in business.

Our story

Anthony Bendele started Bendele Electric twenty years ago with a truck, a license and a simple idea: do the job right, charge a fair price, and answer the phone. It is still a family business, and it still runs that way.

Over the years the work has grown from house calls to plants, packing houses and commercial buildings across the Santa Maria Valley. We still do all three, and we bring the same habits to each.
