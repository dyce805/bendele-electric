Orcutt has a bit of everything: older houses around Old Town, tract homes from the sixties through the eighties, and newer neighborhoods on the south end. The older homes are where we see panels that have run out of room and wiring that was never meant for today’s loads.

We also work for the shops, offices and restaurants along Clark Avenue and Bradley Road, from a single dead circuit to a full tenant improvement.
