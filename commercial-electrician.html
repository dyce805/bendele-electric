Commercial Electrician Santa Maria, CA

Build-outs, lighting, underground and maintenance for offices, shops, restaurants and landlords across the Santa Maria Valley.

Call (805) 287-9044 Get a free quote

Commercial work runs on other people’s deadlines: the lease start, the inspection, the opening day. We give you a date we can keep, coordinate with your general contractor and the other trades, and pass inspection the first time.

We wire new spaces and tenant improvements from the service to the last receptacle, and we look after buildings that are already open: lighting that has gone dark, circuits that are overloaded, panels that have run out of room.

Property managers and owners can call us for one-off repairs or for regular maintenance across several buildings.

What we take on

  • Tenant improvements and office build-outs
  • Restaurant and retail wiring
  • Interior, exterior and parking lot lighting
  • Exit signs and emergency lighting
  • Data and network cabling
  • Service upgrades and new panels
  • Underground conduit and trenching
  • Preventive maintenance and repairs
