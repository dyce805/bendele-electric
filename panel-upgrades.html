Electrical Panel Upgrades Santa Maria, CA

A new panel gives an older house or building the capacity and the protection it was never built with.

Call (805) 287-9044 Get a free quote

The panel is where every circuit in the building starts. When it is too small, too old or simply full, you see it as breakers that trip, lights that dim when the air conditioner starts, or no space left for the circuit you want to add.

We replace fuse boxes and worn-out breaker panels, upgrade the service size when the building needs more power, and add subpanels for shops, additions and detached buildings. Before we quote, we look at what you run today and what you plan to add, so the new panel is sized for both.

A panel change means the power is off for part of a day. We schedule the shutoff with the utility, pull the permit, and meet the inspector, so you are not left chasing paperwork.

Signs it is time

  • Breakers trip often or will not reset
  • The panel is warm, buzzing or shows rust or scorch marks
  • It still uses screw-in fuses
  • There are no open spaces for new circuits
  • You are adding an EV charger, heat pump, spa or addition
  • A home inspector or insurer flagged it
