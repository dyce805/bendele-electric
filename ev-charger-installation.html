EV Charger Installation Santa Maria, CA

A dedicated 240-volt circuit, installed and inspected, so the car is full every morning.

Call (805) 287-9044 Get a free quote

A Level 2 charger runs on a 240-volt circuit, the same kind that feeds a dryer or a range. It charges several times faster than the cord that plugs into a wall outlet, which is the difference between a full battery overnight and a partial one.

We start by checking whether your panel has the capacity for the circuit. If it does, we run the wire, mount the charger or a 240-volt outlet where you park, and label the breaker. If it does not, we will tell you what it takes, whether that is a load-managing charger or a panel upgrade.

We install the charger you already bought or wire for the one you plan to buy, at homes, shops and employee or customer parking.

What we take care of

  • Panel capacity check before any work
  • Dedicated 240V circuit and breaker
  • Hardwired chargers or a 240V outlet
  • Garage, carport, driveway and parking lot locations
  • Trenching to detached garages and lots
  • Permit and inspection
