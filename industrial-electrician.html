Industrial Electrician Santa Maria, CA

Three-phase power, motor controls and equipment hookups for plants, packing houses, cold storage and shops in the Santa Maria Valley.

Call (805) 287-9044 Get a free quote

A stopped line costs more than the repair does. We plan industrial work around your production schedule, show up with the material already on the truck, and stay until the equipment is running again.

We install and service the power side of a plant: the gear that brings power in, the panels and conduit that carry it across the floor, and the starters and drives that run your motors. We also hook up new machines and chase down the faults that keep tripping the old ones.

Every job is done under our California C-10 license, to code, with panels labeled so the next person who opens the door knows what they are looking at.

What we take on

  • New equipment and machine hookups
  • Motor starters, VFDs and control wiring
  • 480V three-phase services and feeders
  • Switchgear, panelboards and transformers
  • Rigid conduit, EMT and cable tray
  • Lighting for warehouses and yards
  • Underground and trenching
  • Troubleshooting, repair and shutdown work
